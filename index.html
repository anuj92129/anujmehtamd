Anuj Mehta, MD

Adult Reconstruction Orthopaedic Surgeon

Dr. Anuj Mehta is an orthopaedic surgeon specializing in adult reconstruction and complex joint replacement. He is currently completing a specialized fellowship in Adult Reconstruction at the prestigious Lenox Hill Hospital in New York City, focusing on primary and revision hip and knee arthroplasty, robotic-assisted surgery, and minimally invasive techniques.

Dr. Mehta completed his orthopaedic surgery residency at Temple University Hospital, where he served as Chief Resident and was recognized on Temple’s 30 Under 30 list for research innovation. He earned his Doctor of Medicine from Penn State College of Medicine, where he was inducted into the Alpha Omega Alpha (AOA) Honor Society and received the Alphonse E. Leure-DuPree Exemplary Medical Student Award. Prior to medical school, he graduated magna cum laude from the Honors Biology program at Temple University.

He is dedicated to advancing orthopaedic care through clinical excellence, surgical precision, and restoring patient mobility.

Clinical Focus & Care Philosophy

"Restoring mobility, relieving pain, and helping patients return to an active, fulfilling life through evidence-based surgical care, advanced technology, and personalized treatment plans."

Specialized Clinical Focus & Surgical Expertise

Advanced fellowship training in complex hip and knee reconstruction, robotic technology, and minimally invasive techniques.

Hip Arthroplasty & Reconstruction

  • Primary Total Hip Arthroplasty: Direct Anterior and Posterior Approaches
  • Complex & Revision Hip Replacement: Management of hardware failure, infection, instability, and severe bone loss
  • Conditions Treated: Hip Osteoarthritis, Avascular Necrosis (AVN), Post-Traumatic Arthritis, and Hip Dysplasia

Knee Arthroplasty & Reconstruction

  • Primary Total Knee Arthroplasty (TKA): Advanced component alignment strategies and joint restoration
  • Partial (Unicompartmental) Knee Replacement: Targeted preservation of native joint kinematics
  • Complex & Revision Knee Replacement: Reconstruction for aseptic loosening, persistent pain, instability, or infection

Surgical Innovation & Technology

  • Robotic-Assisted Surgery: Precision navigation for optimized component positioning and soft tissue balancing
  • Computer-Navigated & Patient-Specific Planning: Pre-operative 3D modeling and custom intraoperative execution
  • Minimally Invasive Techniques & Rapid Recovery: Multimodal pain protocols and perioperative care designed for accelerated rehabilitation

Patient-Centered Care Philosophy

Every patient’s joint health journey is unique. Dr. Mehta emphasizes thorough patient education, transparent communication, and non-surgical options—such as physical therapy, lifestyle modifications, and targeted joint injections—before considering surgical intervention.

When surgery is required, modern surgical guidance, muscle-sparing approaches, and multimodal pain management are leveraged to optimize surgical accuracy, minimize discomfort, and help patients safely regain their independence.
